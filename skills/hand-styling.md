# Hand Identity, Manicure and Fabric Consistency
Track nail length and polish shade, number of rings, left/right wrists, bracelet placement, sleeve type, material and color. Do not suddenly add or remove an accessory across 8-second clips. Two hands cooperate naturally; never create extra fingers or impossible grips. Lightly support the shoe by heel, upper or strap without deforming. Hands should not obscure essential design. Hand-reference photo, when supplied, sets appearance; styling controls are default descriptions when photo absent. Keep human anatomy natural. Do not expose face or upper torso.
